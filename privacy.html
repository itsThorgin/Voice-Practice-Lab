Privacy

Voice Practice Lab analyses your voice in your browser. The app does not upload your microphone audio, recordings, pitch readings or practice results.

Your microphone

The app requests microphone access when you select Start microphone. Your browser controls permission. Select Stop microphone to end capture. Live microphone sound is not played through your speakers.

When you leave the tab in the background, live input and analysis pause. Returning can resume input that you already started, or the app may ask you to select Resume microphone. Recording and playback do not restart automatically.

Recordings and results

Recordings, pitch history and practice results stay in this page session. The app does not save them to browser storage, upload them or download them automatically.

You can keep two recordings, A and B, for local comparison. Each can be up to 60 seconds long. Stopping the microphone keeps completed recordings available. Delete A or Delete B removes that take. Replacing a take removes the previous one. Resetting the session, reloading or leaving the page clears the recordings and practice results.

Saved settings

The app saves your target choices, tuning reference and exercise note range in this browser on this device. These are settings you choose, not measurements of your voice. Each page session starts with No target, even when a target choice was saved.

Metronome, speech pacing and noise settings stay in the current page session only. Speech presets do not analyse your words or measure your speaking rate. Metronome and noise playback stop when you hide or leave the page and require Start again after returning.

  • Clear saved app settings removes saved settings. It keeps your current session. Changing a setting later saves it again.
  • Reset current session stops audio and microphone capture and clears recordings, pitch history and practice results. It keeps your current target and settings.
  • Delete all app data stops audio and capture, clears the session and saved settings, and restores defaults after you confirm.

If the browser cannot save or remove settings, the app shows a message. These controls affect this app's data. They do not clear browser permissions, browser history or data from other websites.

Website requests

The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, trackers, remote fonts or third-party app code. Its practice features do not make network requests.

Loading the website sends normal page and file requests to its host. The host can receive your IP address and browser request details and may keep access logs. This is separate from voice processing in the app.

The browser and operating system manage their own memory and audio buffers. Removing data from the app does not guarantee that every copy in those systems is immediately erased.
